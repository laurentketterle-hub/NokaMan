# Public Learner Corpora & Datasets

A curated index of publicly available ESL/learner corpora suitable for language
assessment research and calibration. All datasets listed include license
information and access links.

| # | Dataset | Languages | Size | Description | License | Link |
|---|---------|-----------|------|-------------|---------|------|
| 1 | **Cambridge Learner Corpus (CLC)** | EN | 50M+ words | Exam scripts from Cambridge English exams, error-coded and annotated with CEFR levels. | Research (apply) | [cambridge.org](https://www.cambridge.org/gb/cambridgeenglish/better-learning-insights/CLC) |
| 2 | **EF-Cambridge Open Language Database (EFCAMDAT)** | EN | 1.2M scripts | Open-access corpus of learner writing from Englishtown, annotated with CEFR levels and L1 metadata. | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | [uclouvain.be](https://www.uclouvain.be/en/research-institutes/ilc/cecl/efcamdat.html) |
| 3 | **International Corpus of Learner English (ICLE)** | EN (16 L1s) | 3.7M words | Argumentative essays by upper-intermediate/advanced university learners from 16 mother-tongue backgrounds. | Research (apply) | [uclouvain.be](https://www.uclouvain.be/en/research-institutes/ilc/cecl/icle.html) |
| 4 | **Japanese Learner Corpus (JLC) / NINJAL** | JA | 6M+ words | Essays and oral narratives from learners of Japanese, tagged for errors and proficiency level. | CC BY 4.0 | [ninjal.ac.jp](https://www.ninjal.ac.jp/english/) |
| 5 | **Korean Learner Corpus (KLC)** | KO | 1.5M words | Writing samples from Korean learners collected by the National Institute of Korean Language, graded by TOPIK level. | Research (apply) | [korean.go.kr](https://www.korean.go.kr/) |
| 6 | **MERLIN Corpus** | DE, IT, CS | 2,290 texts | CEFR-rated learner texts (A1–C1) for German, Italian, and Czech from standardized tests. | CC BY 4.0 | [merlin-platform.eu](https://www.merlin-platform.eu/) |
| 7 | **TOEFL11 Corpus** | EN (11 L1s) | 12,100 essays | TOEFL iBT essays scored holistically (1–5), with native-language metadata (Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Telugu, Turkish). | LDC (purchase) | [catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2014T06](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2014T06) |
| 8 | **Lang-8 Learner Corpus** | Multi (EN, JA, KO, ZH, etc.) | 70M+ sentences | User-submitted journal entries with native-speaker corrections across 90+ language pairs. | CC BY-NC-SA | [lang-8.com](https://lang-8.com/) (archived) |
| 9 | **FCE Learner Corpus** | EN | 2.2M words | First Certificate in English (FCE) exam scripts graded on B2-level Cambridge scale, widely used in AES research. | Research (apply) | [ilexir.co.uk](https://www.ilexir.co.uk/datasets/fce.html) |
| 10 | **TOCFL Learner Corpus** | ZH | 500K+ characters | Essays from the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language, graded by TOCFL band (A1–C2). | Research (apply) | [sc-top.org.tw](https://www.sc-top.org.tw/) |
| 11 | **Spanish Learner Corpus (CEDEL2)** | ES | 1.2M words | Written and spoken data from English-speaking learners of Spanish across all CEFR levels, with native controls. | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | [cedel2.eu](http://cedel2.eu/) |
| 12 | **VIET4 Corpus** | VI | 300+ texts | Vietnamese learner writing samples from university-level ESL students, annotated with error tags and CEFR estimates. | CC BY 4.0 | Contact authors (VNU-HCM) |

## Usage Notes

- **Licensing**: Always verify the current license terms before integrating a corpus into a
  product. Some corpora require formal application or institutional affiliation.
- **Attribution**: Cite the dataset authors when using these resources in research or
  calibration workflows. Example citations are available on each corpus homepage.
- **Restrictions**: No restricted or paywalled media is redistributed here. This index
  links to the official distributors only.
- **For NokaMan Calibration**: Corpora with CEFR labels (CLC, EFCAMDAT, MERLIN) are
  the best candidates for calibrating rubrics and validating band-mapping accuracy.
